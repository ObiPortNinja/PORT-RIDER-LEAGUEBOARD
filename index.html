  • Perfect shift: a solo shift with 50+ parcels taken out, or a lead (training) shift with 40+, and 98%+ delivered. On doorstep shifts you also need 95%+ inside InPost's delivery window (EDW). Shifts still waiting for their scores don't count yet, and don't break a streak.
